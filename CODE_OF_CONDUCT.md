# Code of Conduct

Lexikon follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and any other space related to Lexikon.

In short: be respectful and kind, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project and its community. Harassment,
insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, tell the maintainers,
[@julianel5](https://github.com/julianel5) and [@joaquinmuzzi](https://github.com/joaquinmuzzi),
privately: use GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment, or open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/julianel5/Lexikon/security/advisories/new) and
describe what happened. Reports are handled privately and as quickly as possible, and the person
who reports is protected from retaliation.

The maintainers decide on the consequences following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant: from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
